# SickB genesis dedication: mainnet block 10

The founder wrote this dedication on May 29, 2026. On June 3, 2026, it was deployed as the **first deliberate application transaction on SickB Chain mainnet**, at block **10** (chain ID **5016**).

This page preserves the **complete message text and transaction readout supplied by the founder on November 25, 2026**, consistent with the historical Phase 3 mainnet launch record. It is a **source-backed historical record**, not a claim that this repository independently queried the live RPC today.

> It is May 29th now... I should be able to launch before or on June 23rd... My 50th birthday... I remember setting up my first purchase of Litecoin over a decade ago, never did I think I would eventually create something like this. Never did I expect to learn so much about crypto technology or building a chain that can tick on when my last breath is cast. The blocks can drop, and the goal can be reached. It has been a grind. I get a little emotional here writing this message. I appreciate you all who stuck with me, believed in me, and cheered me on. Never forget, "All roads lead to SickB" and this community that will build from here into the future needs you to tell the story. If we survived the last year there is no reason, we don't survive the next. I am truly grateful, I may be on my motorbike or in the Sick Buffalo bar, but you all will always fill my life with joy. _ Josh ... Wen Motorbike?
